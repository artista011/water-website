01 / THE IDEA

Simple by nature.
Made for the internet.

WATER takes inspiration from something everyone recognizes: a single drop of water. The project explores playful online community culture, clean design, and learning about tokens on Solana. It is not a water charity or an environmental initiative.

IdentityMinimal

One iconic droplet, designed to be recognizable at any size.

CultureCommunity

Memes, creativity, and an open invitation to follow the experiment.

NetworkSolana

Development and testing on Devnet only at this stage.
